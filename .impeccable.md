## Design Context

### Users
Cantonese-speaking lifters (and bilingual gym partners) checking exercise form on a phone between sets. Attention window is about 20 seconds, often one-handed, under gym lights. Job to be done: see the sheet, the kg, the minutes, and a looping Form Agent, then get back to the rack.

### Brand Personality
Inked, street-level, exacting. Speaks like a Hong Kong gym 通告, not a coaching SaaS. Cantonese first. Warm, precise, no cheerleading.

### Aesthetic Direction
Paper lab around the existing vermillion-and-ink step cards. Cream field, chop-red accent, brass only for kg, Noto Sans HK + Big Shoulders Display. Light theme. Anti-references: K-idol pink photocards, gradient headlines, mint callouts, Inter/Outfit/Cormorant, hidden mobile nav.

### Design Principles
1. The step card is the product; chrome must look like the same sheet.
2. One accent (vermillion). Neutrals tint toward it.
3. Every control has hover, focus-visible, and press. Keyboard is a first-class gym user.
4. Instructional motion (Form Agent) stays; decorative drift yields to reduced motion.
5. Persist locale, load level, and the open card so a shared link and a mid-workout refresh still work.
